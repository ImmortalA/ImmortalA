About

I work on robotics problems that sit at the intersection of learning, control, and real-world system implementation. My interests include humanoid and quadruped locomotion, reinforcement learning, imitation learning, robot perception, and sim-to-real transfer. I enjoy building complete systems, from simulation environments and training pipelines to embedded interfaces and physical deployment.

Featured Robotics Work

A few areas that define the kind of robotics systems I like to build.

Humanoid Walking

Developing a humanoid locomotion pipeline using motion retargeting, tracking objectives, and human motion as reference trajectories for learning-based walking.

RL for Legged Robots

Training blind and vision-based locomotion policies in IsaacLab, with emphasis on terrain robustness, stable gait formation, and scalable parallel simulation.

Robot Perception and Systems

Building practical robotics software with ROS, RGB-D sensing, embedded communication, actuator interfaces, and deployment-aware system design.
